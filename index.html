About the occupancy window. For an Originating train, the platform is shown occupied from 30 minutes before its scheduled departure (rake brought in from the maintenance line) — this start time is an estimate, marked est. For a Terminating train, the platform is shown occupied until 20 minutes after its scheduled arrival (rake taken to the maintenance line) — this end time is also an estimate. For a Passing train, both the arrival and departure shown are the station's own confirmed times, so no estimate is needed. These 30/20-minute figures are typical placeholders, not figures pulled from either source file — actual buffer times vary by train and rake availability, so treat them as a planning approximation and confirm exact clearance times locally before relying on them operationally.

Source. Scheduled times, train names and running days come directly from the station's train-wise berthing list (w.e.f. 07.09.2026). Platform capacity (shown next to each platform heading) is carried over from the separate platform-wise chart. Please cross-check against the source documents or current NTES/timetable before using this operationally.
